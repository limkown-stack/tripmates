常见问题

怎么和朋友一起记一趟? 打开旅程, 点右上角「两个人」的按钮, 把链接发给朋友。朋友用自己的 iPhone 打开同行, 选好「这趟里我是哪一位」就加入了。

数据存在哪里? 只在你的手机和你自己的 iCloud 里。删掉重装或换新 iPhone, 登录同一个 Apple 账户, 账目会自动回来。

删错了旅程怎么办? 在「我 > 最近删除」里, 30 天内都可以恢复。

汇率从哪来? 每天从欧洲央行获取参考汇率, 也可以在每趟旅程里自己改。

怎么切换英文? 「我 > 语言」。

FAQ

How do I track a trip with friends? Open the trip, tap the “two people” button at the top right, and send the link. Friends open it in Tripmates on their own iPhone and pick which person they are.

Where is my data? Only on your phone and in your own iCloud. Reinstall or switch to a new iPhone with the same Apple Account and your entries come back.

Deleted a trip by mistake? Restore it from “Me › Recently Deleted” within 30 days.

Where do exchange rates come from? Daily reference rates from the European Central Bank. You can also set your own on each trip.
