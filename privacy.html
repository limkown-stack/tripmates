同行 隐私政策

更新于 2026 年 11 月

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。同行没有自己的服务器, 也没有账号系统, 开发者看不到你的任何数据。

你的数据存在哪里

旅程、每一笔账、转账记录、同行人的名字和头像, 只存在你的手机里, 以及你自己的 iCloud (由 Apple 保管, 只有你和你邀请的人能看到)。

和别人一起记账

你邀请别人加入某趟旅程后, 这趟旅程的内容会通过 iCloud 共享给对方。你可以随时停止共享。

照片和相机

只在你给同行人设头像时使用, 照片缩小后存在你自己的数据里。

网络

联网只为两件事: 同步你的 iCloud, 以及从欧洲央行获取当日参考汇率 (不带任何个人信息)。地图上的地名定位由 Apple 地图完成。

统计和广告

没有任何统计、追踪或广告。

删除数据

删掉 App 会删除手机里的数据; iCloud 里的数据可以在系统「设置 > Apple 账户 > iCloud > 管理存储空间」里删除。

联系

有问题可以在 这里留言。

Tripmates Privacy Policy

Updated November 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Tripmates has no servers of its own and no account system. The developer can't see any of your data.

Where your data lives

Trips, every entry, transfers, and companions' names and avatars are stored only on your phone and in your own iCloud (kept by Apple, and only you and the people you invite can see them).

Share with others

When you invite someone to a trip, that trip's content is shared with them through iCloud. You can stop sharing anytime.

Photos & camera

Only used when you set a companion's avatar. The photo is shrunk and kept in your own data.

Network

The app only goes online for two things: syncing your iCloud, and getting the daily reference rates from the European Central Bank (no personal info is sent). Place lookup on the map is done by Apple Maps.

Analytics & ads

No analytics, tracking or ads.

Delete data

Deleting the app removes the data on your phone. Data in iCloud can be deleted in “Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > Manage Storage”.
